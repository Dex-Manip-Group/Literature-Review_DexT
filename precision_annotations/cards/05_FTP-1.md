## 任务、拓扑与协调

- 任务：generalist tactile policy across sensors/embodiments
- 拓扑：heterogeneous contact-rich tasks; not specifically bimanual load transfer；载荷路径：not central；拓扑变化：implicit
- 角色：task-dependent；协调必要性证据：no inter-hand test

## 传感与部署可实现性

- 触觉：image-, array-, and state-based tactile signals（21 heterogeneous tactile sensors in pretraining corpus；functional areas mapped to morphology-aware token space）
- 训练输入：heterogeneous touch plus robot observations/demonstrations
- 部署输入：sensor-specific encoder outputs in shared MTTS plus policy observations
- 特权信息：训练=simulation sources included but main representation designed for deployable signals；推理=no；未来参考=no；仿真接触=some state-based source modalities may encode simulated contact; source-specific
- 部署判断：deployable_with_sensor_adapter

## 表征与实验

- 表征：morphology-aware heterogeneous tactile token policy；单元：functional-area tactile tokens
- 结构先验：sensor morphology/function-area identities；来源：known sensor/embodiment metadata；动态性：fixed token layout; content dynamic
- 目标：预训练=behavior cloning/policy pretraining across datasets；下游=task finetuning and action prediction；物理目标=none explicit for load flow
- 规模与拆分：~3,000 h, 21 sensors; five downstream hardware configurations；seen/unseen sensor setups and tasks
- 基线/统计：single-source/from-scratch/generalist policy ablations；matched budget=partial; substantial scale advantage is core contribution；seeds=success rates reported; detailed independent seeds NR
- 泛化：对象=task-dependent；传感器=yes, two unseen sensor setups；形态=yes via downstream configurations, mostly finetuned；拓扑=not explicitly

## 三级证据链

1. 作者主张：a unified morphology-aware token space enables cross-sensor and cross-embodiment tactile policy transfer
2. 可观察证据：reported gains on UniVTAC, seen real setups, and two unseen tactile-sensor setups; pretraining ablations
3. 精标判断：支持程度=partial-to-strong。BiCG should borrow morphology/site masks or use FTP-1 as a frozen local encoder, but its innovation must be relational load-flow rather than generic cross-sensor unification.

证据位置：pp.1--4 abstract/method; pp.6--9 Tables 1--3; pp.9--10 limitations; pp.19--22 Appendix Tables 4--6

## 对 BiCG-Rep 的决策

- 影响：strong foundation/local encoder competitor, weak direct competitor on dynamic graph
- 决策：treat as morphology-aware token baseline or optional frozen encoder; do not attempt full 3,000 h reproduction
- 理由：Full reproduction is infeasible for a master's timeline; downstream adapter comparison is informative.
- 主要局限：large heterogeneous proprietary/curated corpus complicates matched reproduction; no explicit inter-hand mechanics or topology transfer
- 未决问题：download completeness; source-by-source licenses; finetuning seeds and budgets
