\subsection{Studies integrated from the 20--26 August update}

The first surveillance round added ten studies. HiTac-WAM and VT-MUSE directly
strengthened predictive and sequential visuo-tactile baselines
~\cite{xue2026hitacwam,xu2026vtmuse}. ViTacPhys, fine-grained slip sensing, and a
durable tactile fingertip expanded physical-property, local-event, and hardware-
reliability considerations~\cite{liu2026vitacphys,ma2026slip,cottrell2026durable}.
Explicit physical-interaction events and CoToGrasp clarified the distinction
between demonstrated or prescribed contact structure and topology inferred from
deployment observations~\cite{overbeek2026oneshot,merand2026cotograsp}. Modality
masking and PartialBiGrasp strengthened non-tactile bimanual controls
~\cite{cheng2026modalitymasking,kaura2026partialbigrasp}, while SoftVTBench added
a safety-aware deformable-object benchmark~\cite{jing2026softvtbench}.

\subsection{Studies integrated from the 27--30 August update}

The second round added seven studies. MeshPriorDiT motivated a three-way
comparison among structured prior, expressive residual, and their hierarchy
~\cite{wang2026meshpriordit}; relaxation-aware soft-gripper sensing motivated
contact-duration and thermal/viscoelastic drift tests~\cite{wang2026relaxation}.
Robot juggling and CLAP strengthened rapid online-dynamics and cross-embodiment
video-world-model controls without supplying distributed touch
~\cite{lee2026robotjuggling,liu2026clap}. TemporalFlow-VLA and PredVLA motivated
history-order interventions and exact open-loop prediction-error ablations
~\cite{yang2026temporalflowvla,sawada2026predvla}. FLARE made failure detection,
local retry, and reset/recovery separate evaluation outcomes
~\cite{zhao2026flare}.

\subsection{Interpretive and artifact boundaries retained}

The integrated update changes what constitutes a credible baseline, but it does
not upgrade every record to full-text precision evidence. In particular:

\begin{itemize}
  \item ``contact topology'' in grasp synthesis can denote a requested static
  layout; it is not automatically a dynamic, observation-inferred task graph;
  \item geometry-supervised temporal history, visual world models, and visual--
  language recovery remain non-tactile controls unless distributed touch is an
  actual deployment input;
  \item the ViTacPhys artifact remained forthcoming at the audit date, while the
  public SoftVTBench release and current manuscript described different dataset
  scales; and
  \item CLAP provided a public code/checkpoint entry, but artifact availability
  does not by itself establish tactile or load-topology evidence.
\end{itemize}

Future updates should append a dated row, record changes in publication and
artifact status, and state explicitly whether the manuscript corpus, the
precision subset, or both were changed. Counts based on the 30-paper precision
audit must retain that denominator until new papers receive the same schema,
located-evidence card, and quality checks.

\subsection{Studies integrated in the 7 October catch-up}

This pass covers sources available on 7 October for first releases after
30 August, plus ContactWorld, first posted in June and discovered through its
September revision. Fourteen new archive records received located full-text
audits and narrative integration; the 30-paper precision schema and its August
completion date are unchanged. The manuscript corpus is refrozen at 105 records.
The 86-PDF August cache remains a historical snapshot: only CALIPER, CADeT, and
DemoMimic PDFs were separately validated in this pass; other new full texts were
read in official HTML. Metadata-only leads and the inaccessible current VT-MUSE
revision were not used to change study-level claims.

BiView-Touch and temporal-compliant handover narrow the previous gap statement
by providing direct, differently scoped cross-hand representation and control
evidence~\cite{liang2026biviewtouch,marra2026temporaltactilehandover}. CADeT adds
observable transmission-mode inference with active probing, while DemoMimic and
Bench2Dex strengthen geometry and benchmark controls
~\cite{hu2026cadet,sharma2026demomimic,yang2026bench2dex}. Dex-X makes retained
reference conditioning explicit~\cite{chen2026dexx}. ME-Dex 1.0, DexTouch-WM,
UVTA, and ContactWorld distinguish tactile supervision, future conditioning,
representation information, and actual feedback dependence
~\cite{zhang2026medex,qin2026dextouchwm,li2026uvta,zhang2026contactworld}.
RATE separates proxy-safe success from calibrated physical safety; CALIPER
strengthens perturbation-validity controls
~\cite{jiang2026rate,mehta2026caliper}. SimpleTouch and VISTA, both first posted
on 2 October, add strong pretrained-token and pose-conditioned spatial controls
while exposing feedback-frequency and finite-group assumptions
~\cite{yang2026simpletouch,wong2026vista}.

All 14 additions are coded as preprints or author-claim records. Workshop
presentation, submission, and author-reported acceptance were not upgraded to
independently verified venue status. The inherited TactiDex author-page
acceptance is also reassigned to the author-claim tier, retaining its raw status. The conclusion now targets the conjunction
of deployable robotic load evidence, matched structural benefit, and
contact-topology-defined transfer, rather than absence of relational touch or
inferred transmission modes. No expanded-corpus zero count is asserted.

\subsection{21 October integration and revision boundaries}

Fifteen new studies receive located full-text archive audits, not precision-core
promotion. Fourteen official HTML texts and one PDF support the additions;
ForeTac and ILA exact-version PDFs support separate revision comparisons.
The manuscript now uses 136 records, while precision coding remains dated
13 August with 30 papers. The dated ledger separately records same-day boundary
and unaudited leads, selected version-history coverage, and artifact limits.

The central joint gap is retained, with stronger positive comparators:
binary-feedback necessity, real local bimanual control, outcome-aligned tactile
world modelling, and genuine kinematic link-removal transfer are now explicit.

load inference plus contact-topology transfer. Pretraining, online touch,
demonstration context, future recorded signals and control cadence receive
separate interpretations. ForeTac v2 changes four-class quality scoring to
binary good/bad scoring without changing reported tables; ILA v2 only removes
a premature conference header from otherwise matching extracted scientific text.
Both remain preprints; the historical 14 October audit is preserved.
