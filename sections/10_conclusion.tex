\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Limitations of this review}

This review is intentionally broad but not exhaustive. The archive was assembled
for a concrete research decision, which biases it toward dexterous hands,
learning-based control, recent tactile foundation models, and graph/contact
representations. It under-samples classical grasp mechanics, human haptics,
teleoperation, non-learning force control, and some industrial dual-arm systems.
The synthesis does not use dual independent screening or extraction. The
86-PDF cache is an August historical record; the 7 October pass verified
three new PDFs; the 14 October pass uses fourteen official HTML full texts and two official PDFs, without a full cache
refresh. The 21 October pass adds fourteen HTML audits and one PDF audit, with
two existing-paper version comparisons. Five manifest entries remain source-only, and publication/code/data
status can change rapidly.
Several 2026 methods are preprints or accepted/programme-listed works; their final
claims and artifacts may differ. Thirty purposively selected core papers received
full-text precision coding, while 105 scholarly records remain outside that
schema: 45 have located
full-text archive audits across the October updates and 60 retain the older lighter
archive-wide coding layer. The precision subset is not a
random sample, and neither layer used dual independent extraction or inter-rater
agreement. Transparent evidence cards reduce ambiguity but do not replace those
controls or independent reproduction.

The corpus is too heterogeneous for meta-analysis. Reported success rates mix
different embodiments, sensors, training budgets, objects, horizons, and
definitions. Counts therefore describe only the curated archive. The review also
does not claim that load paths are the only useful abstraction. Deformation,
compliance, friction cones, contact fields, hybrid modes, and action-conditioned
latent dynamics may be superior in particular regimes.

The archive was initially frozen at 74 records on 12 August, updated to 91 on
30 August, and refrozen at 105 on 7 October 2026. That catch-up added
13 in-window first releases and one older work discovered through a September
revision. Metadata-only watchlist leads and an unread VT-MUSE revision remain
outside the new synthesis. This improves recency without making the search
exhaustive. No surveillance addition was retroactively treated as part of the
30-paper precision subset. The 14 October surveillance integrates 16 additional
full-text archive records and refreezes the narrative at 121; the precision
denominator and its historical date remain unchanged. The 21 October pass
adds 15 full-text audits and two existing-paper revision checks, integrates their
interpretation, and refreezes the manuscript at 136 records.

\subsection{Conclusion}

Tactile robotics and bimanual manipulation have each advanced rapidly, but their
intersection exposes an incompletely validated representational layer. Local
tactile encoders
identify what happens at a sensor; bimanual coordination requires a model of how
several interfaces become coupled through shared entities and how that coupling
changes at contact transitions. Graphs, spatial anchors, contact fields, modes,
and unstructured temporal attention are competing ways to express this layer.
None should be presumed best.

The August additions made the comparison more demanding. Directed tactile
prediction, sequential visuo-tactile fusion,
physical-property latents, mesh-prior residual dynamics, visual world models,
prediction-error recurrence, and failure-aware recovery now provide stronger
alternative explanations for performance. A contact-flow model must isolate its
benefit from those mechanisms and show that the benefit comes from deployable
cross-interface evidence.

The new full-text evidence requires a substantive narrowing. Cross-hand tactile
representation learning is already demonstrated in offline human recordings;
causal force history plus compliance is an effective real bimanual handover
baseline; and visual transmission-mode inference with physical probing is already
possible~\cite{liang2026biviewtouch,marra2026temporaltactilehandover,hu2026cadet}.
The unresolved contribution is the joint validation of observation-grounded
robotic cross-interface loads, matched representation benefit, and transfer under
a declared change in contact participants or edges. Neither an offline predictive
dependency nor a predefined mode posterior alone establishes that conjunction.

The world-model and early-October evidence also weakens architecture-only claims.
Predictive tactile supervision can help even when its output is absent from
control, and strong scores can coexist with little measured dependence on current
touch~\cite{zhang2026medex,li2026uvta,yang2026simpletouch}. Geometry-preserving
world-model controls and pose-conditioned equivariant fusion must be matched for
sensor information, goals, and feedback frequency
~\cite{zhang2026contactworld,wong2026vista}. A generated tactile future, a good
surrogate evaluator, or a risk-related objective is therefore a hypothesis to
test, not direct evidence of physical load propagation or calibrated safety
~\cite{qin2026dextouchwm,jiang2026rate}.

The 14 October evidence reinforces, rather than reverses, this narrowed claim.
Temporal-role conditioning, predictive guidance, latent action targets, contact
references, stage-specific force experts, and directional compliance supply
stronger alternatives~\cite{chen2026pace,chen2026foretac,zheng2026ila,
nie2026ctp,huang2026deftvla,yuan2026fomodex}. Real cooperative tool execution is
present, but does not by itself validate independently measured cross-interface
loads. Canonical human-touch alignment and larger datasets must also be separated
from matched-budget online tactile dependence. Future evaluations should match
reference access, auxiliary experience, routing assistance, feedback timing and
controller compliance before attributing a gain to contact structure.

The 21 October evidence further strengthens compact tactile-feedback, local
bimanual-control, and multimodal world-model baselines. Same-checkpoint tactile
removal in blind Baoding supports runtime dependence on a small binary signal;
ContactPolicy and PhysCanvas establish additional real bimanual evidence, not
independently calibrated load-path inference
~\cite{update2026blindbaoding,update2026contactpolicy,update2026physcanvas}.
DexGraspFlow's unseen finger-removal tests are important kinematic-topology
transfer, distinct from a declared holdout of inferred contact participants or
edges~\cite{update2026dexgraspflow}. These findings retain the narrowed joint gap,
but require a more explicit separation of morphology, sensed contact, control
cadence and pretraining budget. ForeTac v2 changes its quality objective rather
than its headline outcomes; ILA v2 removes a premature conference header, so
neither revision justifies a publication-status upgrade.

The strongest immediate benchmark is quasi-static handover because it provides
observable phases and controlled load redistribution. Cooperative tool--workpiece
manipulation is an established but complementary extension that adds a longer and
role-asymmetric load path. The narrower question of matched, observation-grounded
cross-hand load flow is comparatively sparse in the mapped corpus; it belongs
after the simpler mechanism has passed
matched-budget and coordination-necessity gates. Across both tasks, the decisive
methodological requirements are deployment-observable inputs, explicit label
provenance, episode/object/contact-sequence splits, oracle--inferred--random
structure comparisons, uncertainty, and interventions.

The full-text core reinforces this requirement: zero of 30 papers evaluated
strict held-out contact-topology transfer under deployment-observable inference.
This remains a historical scoped result for that fixed subset, not a zero count
for the 135 scholarly works in the expanded archive or the entire literature,
and it motivates a decisive experiment rather than a priority claim.

An explicit bimanual contact-flow encoder can therefore be a strong contribution,
but the graph layer is not the contribution. The contribution is a falsifiable
factorization of sensed interfaces and object-mediated load propagation, together
with evidence that this factorization improves prediction, control, or transfer
under conditions where simpler temporal fusion does not. That framing provides a
clear starting point for experiments and a durable standard against which future
structured tactile representations can be judged.
